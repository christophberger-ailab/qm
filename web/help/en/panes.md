# The panes beside the editor

Next to the editor you can show up to three panes. Their buttons are in the content header, above the editor.

- `Preview` shows the [Markdown preview](preview.md).
- `Copyedit` shows the [copyediting tool](copyedit.md).
- `Help` shows this help.

Each button shows or hides its pane. They work independently, so you can have none, one, or several panes open at once. A pressed button means the pane is shown.

## Swapping panes

When more than one pane is shown, each has a head with its name. Drag one pane's head onto another pane to swap the two. The order is remembered.

## The help pane

The help pane shows these pages. Links between help pages open in the pane itself; a link to a web site opens in a new browser tab. Above the page you find:

- `◀ Back` returns to the help page you read before.
- `Contents` brings you to the table of contents.
- The dropdown jumps to any help topic.
- The language menu switches the help between English and German. The help starts in your system's language when it has that language, and in English otherwise; once you pick one, it keeps your choice.

The pane remembers the page you were reading, also across sessions.

The `Help` button is there even when no page is open, so you can read the help before you open a project or select a page.

## Resizing

Every pane has a divider on its left side.

- The divider next to the editor changes the width of the pane and the editor.
- A divider between two panes moves width from one pane to the other and leaves the editor as it is.

The tree on the far left has a divider of its own as well.

## Remembered

Which panes are shown, their order, and their widths are remembered across page switches and sessions.
